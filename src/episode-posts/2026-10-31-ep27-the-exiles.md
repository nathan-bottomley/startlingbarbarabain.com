This month, the remaining crew of Moonbase Alpha discover, to their horror, that the fifty missiles orbiting their planet are apparently full of blonde young people from children's TV — the titular exiles, who are determined to return home and teach their elders a valuable lesson about animal cruelty and how to make spaceships out of egg cartons and sticky-back plastic. Inevitably, they intend to do this in a way that will kill everyone on the base, and so Helena has no alternative but to pull a particularly vigorous face before springing into action.
